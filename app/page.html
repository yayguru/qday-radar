📡 QDay Radar

The first time a wallet sends a transaction, its secp256k1 public key becomes public forever. On Q-Day a quantum computer can turn that key into the private key. QDay Radar checks 7 EVM chains for that exposure and lets you anchor a hybrid-signed (ECDSA + ML-DSA-87) record and a post-quantum successor on QoreChain.

Try vitalik.eth · burn address · read-only, nothing is stored.

🛡 Your post-quantum account (QoreX)

🔗 Link exposed wallet → PQ successor

1) Sign the migration message with the exposed wallet (MetaMask etc.). 2) Your PQ account submits linkSuccessor — hybrid: the legacy ECDSA signature is checked on-chain and the sender must hold a registered ML-DSA key.
